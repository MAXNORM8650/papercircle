\section{Introduction}
Research communities face a growing burden of literature discovery and synthesis. Even well-scoped queries can yield hundreds of results across heterogeneous sources with inconsistent metadata and varying quality. Paper Circle addresses this problem with a multi-agent discovery pipeline that combines structured query expansion, multi-source retrieval, scoring, and diversity-aware ranking. The system is integrated into a collaborative reading platform, enabling communities to share, discuss, and track the lineage of research outcomes.

This paper focuses on the discovery and analysis subsystem. We present the agent architecture, describe the retrieval and scoring methods, and document the structured outputs that make the pipeline auditable and reproducible. Unlike ad-hoc search tools, Paper Circle produces synchronized JSON, CSV, BibTeX, Markdown, and HTML exports after each agent step, enabling downstream tooling for curation and analysis.

Contributions. This paper makes the following contributions: (1) a deterministic multi-agent discovery pipeline that combines offline and online retrieval with explicit state management and reproducible artifacts at each step; (2) a structured export interface that synchronizes JSON, BibTeX, CSV, Markdown, and HTML outputs across agent actions for auditing and reuse; (3) a multi-criteria scoring and diversity-aware ranking framework tailored for research discovery rather than question answering; and (4) built-in step-level evaluation metrics for longitudinal analysis of discovery pipelines.
