\section{Limitations}
Several limitations follow from the current implementation. Authority scoring uses heuristic proxies (recency and source weighting) rather than citation counts or peer-review status. Offline retrieval depends on the quality and freshness of the local corpus, and metadata completeness varies across sources. Some scoring components rely on TF--IDF representations, which may underperform on highly technical or short abstracts. Finally, while the system provides evaluation metrics, it does not ship with a standardized benchmark dataset; rigorous comparative evaluation requires external ground truth construction.
